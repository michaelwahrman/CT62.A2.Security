CT62 Synagogue Security

Prototype - version A2

This is an untested prototype. It is not a security system and must not be relied on to protect anyone.

I hope this work will eventually lead to a security system for synagogues. But it isn't one yet. Everything shown here - people, cameras, recognition and alerts - is simulated.

To go further I need cameras, image material and other things to test with. I do not have them and I cannot afford them.

If you would like to contribute any of these, please let me know. But please do not make stupid demands or cause me any trouble. Thank you.
